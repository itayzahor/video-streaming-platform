# App Overview
Our app is a dynamic video-sharing platform designed with a seamless user experience in mind. From the moment users land on the Home Page, they can easily browse through a list of videos, use the search bar to find specific titles, and personalize their experience by signing in, signing up, or switching to dark mode. The goal is to make the interface intuitive and engaging right from the start.

Once signed in, users can head to the Watch Video Page, where they can not only enjoy smooth playback with features like pause, skip, and rewind, but also actively engage with the content by leaving comments, liking or disliking videos, and seeing how many others have watched. Interaction is key here, as users can add and delete comments, fostering a sense of community around each video.

The Sign-Up and Sign-In Pages make account creation and login effortless. Users are guided through the process with features like username availability checks and password strength validation, ensuring secure account creation. Once signed in, the Profile Page becomes a personal hub where users can view and edit their username, manage their uploaded videos, and sign out when they're done. Any changes made to their username or videos are instantly reflected across the platform, from the video list to the Watch Video page.

Adding videos is simple and efficient. The Add Video Page allows users to upload new content by providing a video file, title, and thumbnail. Once uploaded, their videos are automatically visible on the Watch Video page, in search results, and in their profile. If users need to update a video, the Edit Video Page gives them the flexibility to change the video’s name or replace the file altogether, with updates appearing everywhere the video is displayed.

For those looking to manage their content, the Delete Video feature allows users to remove any video they've uploaded. Once deleted, the video disappears from the video list, Watch Video page, and profile, ensuring that content management is straightforward and instant.

With all these features, our app prioritizes seamless video management, enabling users to easily upload, edit, and control their content across the platform. The efficient search function and personalized recommendations ensure that users can discover relevant content quickly, while engagement features like comments, likes, and video management help keep the experience interactive and dynamic. All user actions are reflected instantly across the app, ensuring a smooth, integrated experience from start to finish.
